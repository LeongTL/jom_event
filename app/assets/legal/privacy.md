# Privacy policy
Draft for the closed beta, written for Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). The final text is reviewed before launch.
## What we collect
Your name and phone number or Google account, the events you save and book, your chat messages and your reviews. Hosts also give payout account details.
## Your location
Events near you are found with your location rounded to about 1 km. It is used for that search only and is not stored.
## Who sees what
Hosts see the name and phone number you give when you book. Reviews show your first name only.
## Deleting your data
Profile, then Delete account, erases your profile, chats and saved events and removes your sign-in. Bookings and payment records stay for accounting, with your name and phone number blanked.
