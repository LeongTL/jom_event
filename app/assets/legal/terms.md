# Terms of use
Draft for the closed beta. The final text is reviewed before the public launch.
## What Jom is
Jom lists local events run by independent hosts. Jom is a platform: the host, not Jom, runs each event and is responsible for it.
## Booking and refunds
Each host chooses a cancel policy, shown on the event page before you book. Cancel inside the free window and you are refunded to your original payment method. A guest the host does not check in is a no-show and is not refunded.
## Payments
During the beta, payments are test payments and no real money moves. Prices and fees shown are illustrative until the payment gateway goes live.
## Behaviour
Be kind. Do not post phone numbers, links or contact details in event descriptions, chats or reviews. Hosts and guests who break these rules can be removed.
